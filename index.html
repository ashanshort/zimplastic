About Zim Plastic & Poly Industries

A plastic and polymer packaging manufacturer based in Kaliakair, Gazipur.

Zim Plastic & Poly Industries is a manufacturer of poly bags, plastic films and flexible packaging products, operating from Kaliakair Road, Gazipur, Bangladesh.

We supply packaging materials to businesses across Bangladesh — including retail, industrial, agricultural and food sectors — with a focus on consistent quality and reliable delivery.

Our production team operates bag-making machines and extrusion equipment to meet bulk and custom orders. We work with clients who need standard poly bags as well as those with specific size, thickness or printing requirements.
